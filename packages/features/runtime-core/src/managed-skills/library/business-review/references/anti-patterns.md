# Anti-Patterns

Avoid these review failures.

## Turning review into onboarding

Do not create or refresh the full project structure unless the user also needs onboarding. Business Review may update project context later, but its primary job is judgment.

## Asking a questionnaire

Do not ask five generic questions. Ask one focused question that changes the recommendation.

## Treating interest as demand

Separate interest from demand. Positive reactions, compliments, and general curiosity are not demand. Look for switching behavior, urgency, money, time, risk, or repeated workarounds.

## Writing a strategy essay

The user needs sharper judgment and a next move. Keep analysis compact.

## Flattening contradictions

If two facts pull in different directions, preserve the tension. Contradictions are often the most useful part of the review.

## Recommending too many moves

End with one next action. A list of possibilities is weaker than a chosen move with rationale.

## Over-indexing on software

Software delivery is part of running many companies, but not every business review is a build plan. First identify the business bottleneck.

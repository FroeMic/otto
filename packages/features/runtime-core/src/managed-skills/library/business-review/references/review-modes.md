# Review Modes

Pick the review mode that matches the user's current decision. Do not run every mode.

## Idea Review

Use for raw or early business ideas.

Focus:

- customer specificity
- existing alternatives
- urgency
- wedge
- fastest proof

## Strategy Review

Use when the business exists or the direction is mostly chosen.

Focus:

- positioning
- market choice
- sequencing
- differentiation
- tradeoffs

## Offer Review

Use when the user needs to package, price, or explain what they sell.

Focus:

- customer outcome
- scope
- pricing logic
- proof
- what to remove

## Growth Review

Use when the user wants demand, acquisition, retention, or revenue growth.

Focus:

- channel quality
- conversion bottlenecks
- retention loops
- sales motion
- repeatability

## Operating Review

Use for an existing company, team, workflow, or process.

Focus:

- bottlenecks
- owner clarity
- recurring work
- quality control
- operational risk

## Software Review

Use when software delivery is part of the company operating problem.

Focus:

- business objective
- product scope
- build sequence
- release risk
- quality bar
- what not to build yet
